What we do

Our programs

Four areas of work, one mission of compassion. Each program is run with local partners and measured against clear humanitarian standards.

Animal welfare

Rescue, shelter, sterilisation and rehoming — and standing up against cruelty wherever we find it.

Environmental protection

Reforestation, clean-up drives and awareness campaigns that protect habitats for people and wildlife alike.

Women's empowerment

Skills training, livelihood support and safe spaces that help women lead and provide for their families.

Inclusive

Inclusive education

Learning designed for every child, including those with special needs — because education is a right, not a privilege.

Community & shelter

Practical relief and safe spaces for orphans and vulnerable families, delivered with respect and care.

Awareness & advocacy

Campaigns and outreach that change how communities treat animals, the environment and one another.

Our campaign

Introducing the HOPE campus

This is HOPE — the home Yujilife is building for Syria's most vulnerable: shelter, inclusive education, healthcare and second chances, standing on five promises — animal protection, women's empowerment, care for children with special needs, environmental stewardship, and hands-on training.

Animal Protection in action

Every stray has a story. Through rescue, feeding, shelter and veterinary care, Yujilife gives Syria's abandoned dogs and cats a second chance — one safe home at a time.

Our commitment

UN human standards & values

Every program we run is anchored in internationally recognised human-rights principles — with special attention to the dignity of women and children with special needs.

DignityEvery person is treated with respect, regardless of ability or circumstance.
InclusionNo child is left out — accessibility is built into how we teach and care.
EqualityEqual opportunity and voice for women in every community we serve.
ProtectionSafeguarding the vulnerable is at the heart of everything we do.

Our pledge

  • Uphold the rights of children with special needs to learn and thrive
  • Champion the safety, health and empowerment of women
  • Act transparently and account for every resource entrusted to us
  • Treat animals and the environment as part of one shared community of life
