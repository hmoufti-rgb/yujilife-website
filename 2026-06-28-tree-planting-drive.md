Volunteers from across the region came together this June to plant 2,000 native saplings along degraded hillsides near our community sites.

The drive is part of our long-term goal to restore local biodiversity and engage schools and families in hands-on environmental stewardship.

Each tree planted represents a community member who showed up, rolled up their sleeves, and chose to invest in the future.
